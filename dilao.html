Sanji's Fried rice Recipe! (ONE PIECE)

    INGREDIENTS
  • 6 Shrimp-Jen
  • 3 strip Squid
  • 1 Onion
  • 1 Egg
  • Black Sesame
  • 30g Celery
  • 5g Salt
  • 5g Ground White Pepper
  • 5g Corn Starch
  • 2tbsp Chinese Cooking Wine

    Step-By-Step
  • Wash and devein the shrimps. Cut squid into rings. Dice the onion and celery.
  • Separate egg yolk and egg white into two bowl. Beat both well.
  • Mix white pepper, salt, cooking wine and corn starch into the egg white.
  • Bring a pot of water to a boil. Coat the chopped seafood with the egg white mixture. Blanch the seafood in the boiling water. Drain and set aside.
  • Heat up the pan with oil. Add diced celery and onion. Add in rice and salt, stir-fry evenly.
  • Add seafood and stir-fry, pour in the egg yolk. Sprinkle some black sesame seed. Ready to serve.
